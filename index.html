Unlock the World of Coding: ZOR - Your Gateway to Computer Science Learning in Urdu!

کوڈنگ کی دنیا کو دریافت کریں: زور - اردو میں کمپیوٹر سائنس سیکھنے کا آپ کا ذریعہ

Our mission at ZOR, rooted in Pakistan, is to break down language barriers and provide free access to computer science education, in Urdu. In a country where language often hinders the underprivileged from accessing quality learning, and where limited knowledge of instructors can be a hindrance, we strive to bridge the gap. We believe that everyone, regardless of their background, deserves the opportunity to unlock the world of coding. Join us on the journey to make computer science learning accessible to all, fostering a community of empowered minds in the digital age.
